Official Sources & Transparency

Information Transparency & Provenance

Data on this page is compiled from public directories and verifiable government records. BetterAlbay is an independent civic tech project under BetterGov.ph.

Official Source Pointers

  • House of Representatives (20th Congress) — Congressional district representatives.
  • Provincial Government of Albay — Provincial Governor, Vice Governor, and Provincial Board.
  • DILG Barangay Officials Directory — Village-level leadership listings.
  • DILG Local Officials Archive — Municipal and city official profiles.
  • Commission on Elections (COMELEC) — Official election returns and proclamations.

Scope & Limitations Notice

  • This is not a complete list of all government officials in Albay.
  • Barangay officials, SK officials, most ex officio members, judges, and most appointed national- and local-government personnel are not included.
  • The 2025 election returns cited for municipality mayors and vice mayors were labeled partial and unofficial, despite 100% precinct reporting.
  • Most councilors come from a temporary 2025–2028 roster; changes through September 19, 2026 were not individually checked.
  • Names and spellings are transcribed from the previous response and may need validation against original government records before formal use.
  • Looking for village data? Explore our dedicated Albay Barangay Directory.
