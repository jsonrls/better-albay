Ordinance Framework

Municipal ordinances enacted by the Sangguniang Panlalawigan ng Albay

What is an Ordinance?

A municipal ordinance is a local law enacted by the Sangguniang Bayan (Municipal Council) that governs the municipality and its residents. Ordinances have the force and effect of law within the territorial jurisdiction of the municipality.

Ordinances may cover various subjects including but not limited to: taxation, business regulations, public safety, environmental protection, traffic management, and zoning.

Provincial Ordinances on Record

Ordinances of the Sangguniang Panlalawigan ng Albay whose number and official title are preserved in a retrievable public source

What this list is. The Sangguniang Panlalawigan does not publish a machine-readable register of its ordinances, and albay.gov.ph cannot be retrieved by automated clients. Every row below is instead taken from an enacted ordinance text published by the Provincial Government of Albay and preserved by the Internet Archive, and each row links to the document it came from.

What it is not. It is not a complete or current index. Only 6 ordinances could be established from a retrievable source, and the codified Tax Code, Traffic Safety Code, Fishery Code, G.A.D. Code and Tourism Code are listed on the province's own index but their texts were never archived, so their numbers are not shown here. Where a source cites an ordinance without naming the session, the session date reads Not published rather than an inferred date.

These are records of enactment, not a statement of what is currently in force. Confirm any ordinance with the Sangguniang Panlalawigan Secretariat or the Provincial Secretary before relying on it.
